Ballymahon’s Maura Higgins has continued to impress on Dancing With the Stars US after securing the highest score of the night as well as the first ‘9’ awarded by a judge this season.

Maura and her pro dance partner Mark Ballas took to the floor last night for the show’s Mariah Carey-themed episode, performing a waltz to Carey’s emotional track If It’s Over.

DWTS judge Carrie Ann said she became emotional watching the routine, describing it as “artistry in motion”, while fellow judge Derek Hough praised the classic ballroom style of the dance.

The score marks another significant step in Maura’s journey on DWTS as it is her second time to top the leader board.

Despite scoring 21 points for their jazz routine, Ciara Miller and professional partner Brandon Armstrong were eliminated from the competition.
