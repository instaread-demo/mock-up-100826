The 2024/25 MSL National Cup winners and six-time champions Killester will host Limerick Eagles in round one, who are entering the competition as Domino's Men's Division One and Domino's MD1 National Cup champions, with the winners to face EJ Sligo All-Stars.

“As defending champions, it's always nice getting a 'bye',” said Killester head coach Mark Grennell. It gives us that bit more time to be ready and hopefully get the longer term injured back. It will be a tough game away to either Limerick Celtics or VSC Cavan Eagles.”

Last season’s finalists Garvey’s Tralee Warriors will journey to Limerick Celtics, who won the Domino’s MD1 National Cup in 2025, where the winner will face Griffith College Éanna. St. Vincent’s have received a bye to the quarter-final, where they will face either Energywise Ireland Neptune or Belfast Star from the round one fixture.

Reigning Domino’s WD1 champions Limerick Sport Huskies have received a bye to the quarter-final, where they will play the winners of Clunetech Kilkenny Stars and Moy Tolka Rovers.

Two Dublin derbies will unfold in round one, as Dublin Raiders face Abbey Seals Dublin Lions and Templeogue play Swords Thunder.

Domino’s Women’s Super League champions Trinity Meteors and last years Domino’s WSL National Cup finalists FloMAX Liffey Celtics will repeat their Domino's WSL National Cup semi-final in the opening round, with Trinity Meteors getting home court advantage. The winner of the tie will take on Keith Daly’s Gurranabraher Credit Union Brunell.

SETU Waterford Wildcats and Utility Trust St. Paul’s will face-off in the final eight, with Career Decisions UCC Glanmire and Midlands Park Portlaoise Panthers set for a quarter-final in Cork.

Last year’s finalists Drogheda Wolves will make the short journey south to face Dublin Lions in the Domino’s MD1 National Cup, and Flexachem KCYMS and Carrick Cruisers await the qualifiers from the Men’s Division Two preliminary cup.
