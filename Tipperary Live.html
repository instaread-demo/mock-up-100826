Padraig Fitzgerald has been appointed as Teagasc Regional Advisory Manager in Tipperary following the retirement of Donal Mullane.

Mr Fitzgerald graduated from University College Dublin (UCD) with a Degree in Agricultural Science. He holds a post-graduate Diploma in Leadership Development Programme and holds a post-graduate Diploma in Freshwater Quality Monitoring and Assessment, both from University College Cork.

He is currently working as a Sustainability advisor with the ASSAP based in Kilmallock County Limerick. Previously he worked as a dairy and drystock advisor in both Limerick and Kerry. In his early career he worked as a REPS planner, having joined Teagasc in 2004.

He is not the only new appointment in the Teagasc ranks, as Enda Geoghegan has been appointed as Regional Manager in Galway/Clare.

The appointments were approved at the Teagasc Authority meeting today, Wednesday 7 October. Both men will lead the Teagasc teams in the respective regions, delivering advisory services to farmers and educational courses through the advisory offices.

Congratulating Padraig and Enda on their appointments, Head of the Teagasc Advisory Service, Thomas Curran said: “I look forward to working with Enda and Padraig as part of the management team in the Advisory programme. They bring a wealth of experience and expertise, and I wish them the best in their new roles.”

The vacancies arose following the retirements of Donal Mullane and Pat Clarke.

Donal Mullane worked as an advisor before he was Principal in Clonakilty Agricultural College.

He was then appointed as Manager of the Tipperary Advisory region.

Pat Clarke had worked as a lecturer in Kildalton Agricultural College before working as an enterprise specialist in Teagasc. He then became Manager of the Galway/Clare advisory region.

Tom Curran thanked both Donal and Pat for their contribution to Teagasc, both in advisory and education, during long and distinguished careers.
