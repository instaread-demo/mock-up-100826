Police probing two overnight arson attacks in Newtownhamilton

  • by

    The Newsroom
  • October 7, 2026
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:23
Instaread

Police are appealing for information and witnesses in relation to two arson attacks in Newtownhamilton last night, Tuesday, October 6.

Sergeant Wilson said: “Shortly  before midnight, officers attended a report of a fire at a house at School Terrace in Newtownhamilton together with colleagues from the Northern Ireland Fire and Rescue Service, who managed to extinguish the blaze.

“Smoke damage was caused to the entire property, however thankfully, there were no reports of any injuries.

“At this stage, it is believed that the fire was started deliberately, and our enquiries are ongoing this morning to establish the circumstances of the incident.

“A second report was also received at around midnight that a tractor had been set alight at another property in the Tullyneil Road area.

“The occupants of the house managed to extinguish the fire themselves, and the full cab of the vehicle was burnt out as a result of the incident.

“A car was reported to have been seen leaving the area in the direction of Newtownhamilton.”

Sergeant Wilson added: “As our enquiries continue into both reports this morning, which we believe at this stage may be linked, we are appealing to anyone with any information which may assist us to get in touch.

“The number to call is 101, quoting reference number 1588 of 06/10/26.”

Alternatively, call Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online at www.crimestoppers-uk.org/
